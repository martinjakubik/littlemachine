What is Little Machine?
==
Little Machine is a very, very, very tiny AI.

It is big enough to do some real AI things, but small enough that you can take a peek at its brain.

Little Machine looks at a set of very small black-and-white pictures and can learn to recognize faces.

It runs on your phone or laptop - no data centers, not even a website. Once you have loaded this page, you can turn off your wifi and Little Machine can run and re-run both the training part, and the inference (answer) part.

(Both of these are rare, but the training part is really rare to see running on your phone.)

If Little Machine can both train and infer on your phone, why don't other AI's do that? Well, it's because Little Machine is really not as powerful. It is here to teach, not to write code or paint masterpieces. It's small so it can teach. It's small so you can understand it.

And that is its power.

Making a Data Set
==

The first thing we need in order to train an AI is a data set. Many of today's large language models (LLM) AIs were trained reading the internet, social networks, or scanned books.

But you will train Little Machine by making the data yourself.

This takes three steps.
1. Browse through the tiny images we will show you.
2. Label the ones that look like faces.
3. Click "Start Training!"

Little Machine will figure out which type of images look like faces and will create a model. You can give that model a name like "Little Machine ZX5000-U" and save it.

It typically takes around 2000 - 3000 yes and no labels to make a good Little Machine, so if you don't want to sit here all day, you can also load one of the prepared data sets. But you can make changes to those and see what happens.

Training the AI
==

Once you have a data set you want to try, just click Start Training!

Little Machine will read the data and make a model. This model is the brain of the AI. You can make a bunch and compare them.

Testing the AI
==

Finally, once you have made a model, you can show it new images, and ask if it sees a face.

When making the data set, training the AI, and testing it, you don't have to train it to see faces. Here are other things you can do:

- decide if an image is a star
- decide if an image is more full on the left or right
- decide if an image is a letter

Any yes or no question will do.

Looking at the AI Brain
==

Here's the part that you really don't get to do very often. Little Machine will happily unscrew its own baseball cap and show you its brain.

This will look like a bunch of numbers, but if you change the data set and run the training again, you can compare two brains and see how they change.

Taking the AI Home
==

If you are happy with your AI, you can store it on your phone or laptop and use it again.

This is how big AI's are made, only they're bigger. But once you know how to make a Little Machine, what's stopping you from going out there and putting more pieces together?
